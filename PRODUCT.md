# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players launching Legion from Steam or Itch on desktop and handheld hardware. They move quickly between loadout, matchmaking, and real-time battles, using mouse, keyboard, or controller.

## Product Purpose

Legion is a competitive multiplayer arena game. The client should get players into a trustworthy match quickly, keep the state of networked actions obvious, and recover cleanly from transient service or connection failures.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, arcane, energetic. Copy should be concise and confident, with enough character to belong in the game without obscuring system state.

## Anti-references

Opaque endless loaders, generic SaaS panels, decorative UI that slows play, browser-like chrome, and errors that require players to diagnose infrastructure.

## Design Principles

- Show the current real-time state and the next expected transition.
- Preserve player momentum with automatic recovery and clear manual exits.
- Keep competitive information legible at a glance.
- Make keyboard, mouse, and controller paths equivalent.
- Treat waiting as part of play, not a blank technical state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast at the 1280×720 baseline, visible focus states, controller and keyboard operability, and reduced-motion fallbacks for nonessential animation.
